## Things that look wrong, or need attention

- `ADER`→`URYDL` is WT-connected (q=1.36e-16) but has no defined mean dF/F (single trial, NaN). `measured_sign_wt` = `undefined`.
- 327 of 1151 wt `connected` pairs ALSO pass the authors' equivalence test (q_eq<0.05): their response differs significantly from control but lies within the authors' equivalence margin (q_eq_th = 1.2; ±1.2 × control SD per their code, unverified for this file), i.e. it is significant but small. They are kept as `connected` and flagged `wt_q_and_q_eq_both_below_0.05`. A classification rubric should decide explicitly how to treat them.
- 5 of 357 unc31 `connected` pairs ALSO pass the authors' equivalence test (q_eq<0.05): their response differs significantly from control but lies within the authors' equivalence margin (q_eq_th = 1.2; ±1.2 × control SD per their code, unverified for this file), i.e. it is significant but small. They are kept as `connected` and flagged `unc31_q_and_q_eq_both_below_0.05`. A classification rubric should decide explicitly how to treat them.
- 750 rows involve AWCON/AWCOFF and carry no anatomical, polarity or extrasynaptic values under `awc_policy=unresolved` (ON/OFF ↔ L/R is random per animal).
- RMHR (376 rows) is a head neuron missing from the authors' head list; it is never observed in the atlas (`not_observed`).
- Anatomical coverage is partial: `anat_chem_white` (white_primary=n2u) is null for 12,276 rows and `anat_chem_witvliet` for 12,276. Apart from AWCON/AWCOFF, the head neurons outside `anat_chem_white` are 34: AS1, AVG, DA1, DB1, DB2, DD1, I1L, I1R, I2L, I2R, I3, I4, I5, I6, M1, M2L, M2R, M3L, M3R, M4, M5, MCL, MCR, MI, NSML, NSMR, SABD, SABVL, SABVR, VA1, VB1, VB2, VD1, VD2; outside `anat_chem_witvliet`, 34 (the same set). By the authors' ganglion file (`aconnectome_ids_ganglia.json`) their ganglia are: anterior pharyngeal bulb 13, posterior pharyngeal bulb 7, retrovesicular ganglion 14. `anat_chem_white_whole` (the Varshney compilation Randi used) misses 0 of them.
- 173 WT-connected pairs have their kernel peak at t=0, so rise time = 0: the fitted impulse response is maximal at stimulus onset. Peak times are resolved at 0.2 s (authors' 0–200 s, 1,000-point axis). `measured_latency_wt` is derived from the atlas kernels; the authors' own per-trial rise times are not in the release.
